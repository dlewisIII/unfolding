### language · note

“an entirely different way of perceiving its surroundings”

The original says «совершенно другой организацией восприятия» (a different organization of perception). “Way of perceiving its surroundings” loses the structural sense of organization and adds “surroundings”. The meaning stays close; “a completely different organization of perception” would be nearer to the original.

### language · note

“our senses, sensations, and ideas”

The Russian names three distinct levels: sense organs («органов чувств»), sensations, and mental representations («представлений»). “Senses, sensations” sounds almost synonymous in English, and “ideas” is somewhat weaker than “representations”. Not an error; “sense organs, sensations, and representations” would keep the distinction.

### language · note

“Mathematics as a Universal Tool for Understanding the World”

The Russian subheading says «познания» (knowing, cognition), while the English says “Understanding”. In block 6 «познать реальность» is rendered as “know reality”, so the key epistemic term moves between “know” and “understand”. The choice reads naturally; “Knowing” would only matter if the terminological link is wanted.

### language · note

“certain universal laws and principles underlie material reality”

«Лежат за материальной реальностью» means behind or beyond material reality, while “underlie” means lie beneath it as a foundation, so the spatial metaphor changes. «Мироздания» (of the universe) is also not carried over. The sense is preserved; “lie behind material reality” would be closer and would echo “beyond” in the title.
